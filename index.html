About me

I'm Udit, a student at JECRC University in Jaipur. What started as curiosity about how apps and websites work has turned into a habit of building things to find out.

I like problems that sit between logic and creativity: structuring code so it stays understandable, then shaping the interface so people enjoy using it. I'm comfortable reading documentation, breaking a big idea into small tasks, and shipping the first version before polishing it.

Right now I'm strengthening my fundamentals, building projects to practice them, and looking for chances to collaborate with other students and developers.
